The last one per cent

Thirty hectares of endangered coastal heath and wetland at Brunswick Heads, on Bundjalung country — all that remains of the wallum in Byron Shire.

A living world.

9 Federally listed
threatened species Koala, wallum sedge frog, Mitchell's rainforest snail, regent honeyeater, swift parrot — and four more
12 NSW-listed
threatened species Including the south-eastern glossy black-cockatoo and the long-nosed potoroo
3 Threatened
ecological communities Swamp sclerophyll forest, swamp oak floodplain forest, coastal saltmarsh

A wallum sedge frog cannot be moved to a nicer wetland, because there is no nicer wetland. It breeds only in water this acid, in sedges that grow only in sand this poor. Lose the ground and you lose all of it at once.

The act that keeps Wallum alive
